## 🚀 About Me

I'm a Computer Engineering student interested in the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence, technology, and human interaction**.

Currently pursuing a Master's degree in Informatics Engineering at the **University of Coimbra, Portugal**, after studying Computer Engineering at the Federal Institute of Paraíba (IFPB), Brazil.

I'm particularly interested in:

- 🤖 Agentic AI and Large Language Models (LLMs)

- 📚 AI for Education and personalized learning

I enjoy building projects that explore how AI can become more useful, accessible, and meaningful in people's lives.
